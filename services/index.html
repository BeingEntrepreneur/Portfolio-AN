Questions

Things people usually ask first.

Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not. I build on what you already use, like Google Workspace, Excel and WhatsApp. If you already pay for tools such as Zoho, Odoo, HubSpot, Slack, Freshdesk or Intercom, I can build on those too, so there's nothing new to buy.

Who owns the tool and the data?

You do. Everything is set up in your own accounts, so it stays with you.

Is my business data safe?

Your data stays in your systems. I only access what the task needs, and I'm happy to sign an NDA.

What if something breaks or needs a change later?

You get a simple guide at handover, and I stay reachable for fixes and small tweaks.

How long does it take?

A LinkedIn revamp takes a few days. Most tools take 1 to 4 weeks, depending on the process.

Tell me the process. I'll tell you if it can be a tool.

A short message is enough. Describe what your team does by hand today, and we'll take it from there.
