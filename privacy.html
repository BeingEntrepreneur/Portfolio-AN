Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In short
  • I only collect what you type into the contact form.
  • I use it only to reply to you about your enquiry.
  • I don't sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to any mailing list.
  • This site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.
  • You can ask me to see, correct or delete your details at any time, using the request form below.

Who I am

This website, atulnaik.in, is the personal portfolio of Atul Naik, based in Goa, India. I am responsible for the personal data you share through it (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

What I collect

When you submit the contact form, I collect:

  • Your name and, if you give it, your company name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • What you write about your process, how you manage it today and what you'd like to improve
  • The date and time of your message, and the page address it was sent from

I don't collect anything else. Please don't include sensitive information such as passwords, financial account details or health information in the form.

Why I use it

I use your details only to read your enquiry and get back to you about it, by email or phone, and to keep a record of our conversation. By submitting the form you consent to this use.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are saved to a private Google Sheet in my Google account, and I receive an email notification in my Gmail inbox. Both are provided by Google, whose servers may be located outside India. Only I have access to them.

Who I share it with

I don't sell, rent or share your details with anyone for marketing. The only third parties involved are the services that run this site:

ServiceWhat it does
Google (Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail)Receives and stores your form submission and notifies me
GitHub PagesHosts this website
Google Fonts, cdnjs (Cloudflare)Deliver fonts and animation code to your browser

Like any website, these services may see technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser connects to them. This is handled under their own privacy policies; I don't receive or store it.

I may disclose your details if required by law.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiries for up to 24 months after our last conversation, then delete them, unless we start working together, in which case I keep what's needed for that work. You can ask me to delete your details sooner.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • Tell you what details I hold about you
  • Correct or update them
  • Delete them, or withdraw your consent to me using them

Use the privacy request form below. I will respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with my response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Make a privacy request

For any privacy question or request, fill in this form. I'll reply to the email address you give, and may ask you to confirm it's you before sharing or changing anything.

Request received

Thanks. I've received your request and will reply by email within 30 days.

Changes

If I change how I handle your details, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
