The operator behind the tools

Operations first. Technology second.

My day job is operations excellence in luxury real estate and facilities management: SOPs, ticketing, MIS and the processes that slow teams down. The tools came from that work. I sit with the people doing the task, find the steps that don't need a human, and build around the ones that do.

How I work

Five steps. The first two matter most.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Sit with the people doing the work. Map every step, every file and every handoff before touching a tool.

  2. 02

    Simplify

    Remove steps that exist only because of the old tools. Automating a bad process just makes it fail faster.

  3. 03

    Design

    Design the workflow, the data structure and the screens around the people who will use them daily.

  4. 04

    Build

    Build with practical technology the business already has, using AI-assisted development to move fast.

  5. 05

    Improve

    Test with real data, train the team, measure, and keep refining as new needs show up.
